Welcome to Bijapur

The City of Historical Wonders

Bijapur, officially known as Vijayapura, is a famous historical city in Karnataka, India. It is well known for its magnificent monuments, rich history and beautiful architecture.

Famous Places to Visit

Gol Gumbaz

Gol Gumbaz is the most famous monument in Bijapur. It is known for its huge dome and whispering gallery.

Ibrahim Rauza

Ibrahim Rauza is a beautiful historical monument famous for its impressive architecture.

Jama Masjid

Jama Masjid is one of the important historical mosques in Bijapur.

Why Visit Bijapur?

  • Rich historical heritage
  • Beautiful architecture
  • Famous historical monuments
  • Unique cultural experience
